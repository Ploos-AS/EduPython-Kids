# Exercises 04 – Numbers and mini calculator

These are optional extra exercises. You do not need to complete everything.

Ask for two integers and display their sum, difference and product.

## B – Try another number

Test zero and a negative number.

## C – Bug hunt

Remove one `int()` call. What changes, and why?

## ⭐ Bonus
