# Exercises 10 – Guess with more attempts

These are optional extra exercises. You do not need to complete everything.

## A – Difficulty

Try ranges 1–20, 1–50 and 1–100.

## B – Attempt limit

Give the player at most five guesses.

## C – Debug state

Temporarily print the secret, guess and attempt count. What does this reveal?

## ⭐ Bonus

Let the player choose a difficulty.
