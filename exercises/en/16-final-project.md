# Exercises 16 – Final project

These are optional extra exercises. You do not need to complete everything.

## A – Idea

Write one sentence beginning `I want to make ...`.

## B – Smallest version

Build the smallest version that proves the idea works.

## C – Three improvements

Choose at least three: choices, score, randomness, loops, functions, lists, graphics, or your own idea.
