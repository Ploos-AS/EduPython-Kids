# Exercises 06 – Randomness and dice

These are optional extra exercises. You do not need to complete everything.

## A – Different dice

Create a 6-sided and a 20-sided die.

## B – Two dice

Roll two dice and display the total.

## C – Predict first

Which results are possible from `randint(1, 6)`?

## ⭐ Bonus

Detect and print a message when both dice match.
