# M3.7 – Make it yours! 🎨

## 🎯 Mission

The game works. Now make it stop being **our** game and start being **your** game.

## 🎭 Pick a theme

Maybe you are a space pilot collecting energy, a cat collecting food, or a knight collecting treasure. Or invent something completely different.

**My game is about ____________________.**

## 🎨 Change the look

Experiment with built-in Turtle shapes, `color()`, `shapesize()`, `screen.bgcolor()`, and the window title.

## 🎮 Change the feel

Change one number at a time:

```python
STEP = 20
monster_speed = 8
lives = 3
```

Play after every change. A good game does not have to be as difficult as possible.

## 📜 Make one rule your own

For example:

- targets are worth 2 points
- the monster speeds up at 5 points
- earn an extra life at 10
- win at 15 points

Choose one idea first and make it work before adding more.

## 🧪 Playtest

Let someone else try the game without controlling it for them. Ask what was clear, what was difficult, and what they would change. You choose which advice to use.

## ⭐ Challenge

Make three changes: one visual change, one rule change, and a new name.

## 🐞 Bug hunt

Use this rhythm:

**Change → run → test → keep or undo.**

One small change at a time.

## 🧠 What you learned

- the same Python pieces can make very different games
- numbers and rules change how a game feels
- testing with other people can give you useful ideas
