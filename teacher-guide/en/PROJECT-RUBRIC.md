# Final project – conversation rubric

This rubric has **no grades and no points**. Use it for a short conversation after the final project, not as competition between learners.

## Idea and ownership
**Developing:** can describe part of the idea with support.  
**Independent:** can explain what they wanted to make and choices they made.  
**Exploring further:** made their own changes or experiments beyond the base idea.

## Understanding
**Developing:** recognises some parts of their code.  
**Independent:** can explain the important parts.  
**Exploring further:** can explain how parts work together and what changes when something is edited.

## Testing and debugging
**Developing:** tests when reminded.  
**Independent:** tested several times and fixed at least one problem.  
**Exploring further:** tests different situations and uses results to improve the program.

## Problem solving
**Developing:** needs help splitting a problem up.  
**Independent:** can work on one small part at a time.  
**Exploring further:** tries several solutions and can explain a choice.

## Presentation
**Developing:** can show the program with support.  
**Independent:** can demonstrate it and explain a part they like.  
**Exploring further:** can also describe a bug, an improvement and what they would do next.

These descriptions apply to **this task right now**. They are not grades, permanent levels or labels for the child.
