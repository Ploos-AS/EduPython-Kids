# Language parity

EduPython Kids uses **Norwegian as its primary authoring language** and supports **Norwegian and English as equal first-class course editions**.

This is not a requirement for literal translation. It is a requirement for pedagogical parity.

## Required parity

For each student lesson, both editions should preserve:

- the same learning goals
- comparable difficulty and scope
- the recurring make/run/change/predict/test rhythm
- challenges and debugging practice
- privacy and safety guidance
- prerequisite assumptions
- progression to the following lesson

Examples and names may be localised naturally.

## Completion rule

A new student-facing course milestone is not language-complete until the required Norwegian and English material exists.

Supporting material should also move toward parity. Teacher material, exercises, printable sheets and navigation may be translated in staged batches, but missing translations must remain visible roadmap work rather than being silently treated as complete.

## Validation

Automated checks should verify structural parity where practical. Human review is still required to judge teaching quality and natural language.
